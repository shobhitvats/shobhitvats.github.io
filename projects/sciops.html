Overview — Nucleovir Therapeutics

  • Worked in the Founder's Office as Scientific Operations Intern at Nucleovir Therapeutics India Pvt. Ltd. (AIC–IISER Pune), reporting directly to the Co-Founder & CSO and the Co-Founder & CEO.
  • Built and maintained internal project-management workflows supporting task allocation, milestone tracking, and cross-team deliverable visibility.
  • Structured shared digital records, organisation standards, and internal documentation templates across research and operational tracks.
  • Assisted with configuring the internal NAS storage server, Samba network file sharing, and role-appropriate access permissions.

Context & Operational Scope

Early-stage biotechnology R&D brings wet-lab experimentation, computational biology, and AI development together under tight timelines. Without consistent operational structure, parallel workstreams can quickly develop fragmented task tracking, inconsistent file records, and communication gaps between teams.

Working from the Founder's Office at Nucleovir Therapeutics, my focus was establishing dependable internal systems so scientists and technical contributors could spend less time hunting for updates or files and more time on core research execution.
