TL;DR — Quick Summary

  • Production-ready Streamlit + FastAPI platform for analysing noncovalent interactions (NCIs) in protein PDB structures, born from IISER Pune Masters research on pnictogen, tetrel, and chalcogen bonding.
  • Detects 15+ NCI families including hydrogen bonds, halogen bonds, chalcogen, pnictogen, tetrel bonds, π–π stacking, cation–π, salt bridges, and metal coordination.
  • High-performance core: KD-tree spatial pruning, vectorised geometry, shared-memory parallelism, and optional Numba/Rust kernels — delivering 60% Runtime reduction on the ParamBrahma HPC cluster.
  • Outputs interactive 3D visualisations, Ramachandran plots, interaction network graphs, PDF/PPTX/Excel reports, and a full REST API.
  • Reduced data exploration time by 40% for early users by centralising fragmented datasets and integrating LLM-assisted interpretation.

Problem & Motivation

Noncovalent interactions (NCIs) govern protein folding, stability, catalysis, and ligand binding. Yet existing tools are fragmented — separate utilities for hydrogen bonds or π-stacking, inconsistent geometric thresholds, and zero provenance tracking.

During Masters research at IISER Pune on chalcogen, pnictogen, and tetrel bonding in protein structures, analysing a single structure required stitching incompatible outputs manually. MolBridge unifies all 15+ families under reproducible, literature-grounded presets.

Architecture

Detection Layer

Decorator-based detector registry allowing each NCI family to register independent geometric criteria and parameter presets without modifying core code.

Performance Layer

Vectorised NumPy geometry, KD-tree spatial pruning, task-graph precompute, POSIX shared-memory pools, and optional Numba/Rust kernels.

API Layer

FastAPI REST backend with async batch execution, progress tracking, and JSON/CSV/PDF/Excel exports documented via OpenAPI /docs.

UI Layer

Streamlit app with interactive py3Dmol 3D viewer, Plotly heatmaps, Ramachandran plots, interaction graphs, command palette, and YAML scenario templates.

Interaction Families Detected

  • Hydrogen Bonds (conventional, low-barrier, C5-type)
  • Halogen Bonds (Cl, Br, I, F — sigma-hole)
  • π–π Stacking (face-to-face & edge-to-face)
  • Cation–π & Anion–π Interactions
  • CH–π & Sulfur–π Interactions
  • n→π* Orbital Interactions
  • Chalcogen Bonds (S, Se, Te)
  • Pnictogen Bonds (N, P, As)
  • Tetrel Bonds (C, Si, Ge)
  • Salt Bridges & Hydrophobic Contacts
  • London Dispersion Forces
  • Metal Coordination (Zn, Fe, Mg, Ca, Cu…)
