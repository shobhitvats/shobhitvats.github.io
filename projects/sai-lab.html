Overview — IISER Pune · Sai Lab (Prof. Saikrishnan Kayarat)

  • Pursuing Integrated BS-MS Dual Degree thesis research in the Department of Biology, IISER Pune (Sai Lab; Supervisor: Prof. Saikrishnan Kayarat; TAC: Prof. M. S. Madhusudhan).
  • Derived small-molecule geometric baselines across >1.2M Cambridge Structural Database (CSD) entries to separate in-plane σ-hole chalcogen contacts from orthogonal H-bonds and survey Group 15 pnictogen and Group 14 tetrel interactions.
  • Scaled headless gemmi / Biopython / cKDTree pipelines across quality-gated experimental PDB structures and 214M AlphaFold DB v4 models on the ParamBrahma HPC cluster (60% runtime reduction).
  • Validated motif enrichment with 100,000-walk kinematic polymer null models, mapped human CX4C motifs against 10,518 ClinVar variants, built a wet-lab NMR/purification protocol bridge, and engineered MolBridge.

Scientific Question & Multi-Scale Validation Strategy

Beyond classical hydrogen bonding and hydrophobic packing, σ-hole interactions mediated by chalcogen (S, Se, Te), pnictogen (N, P, As), and tetrel (C, Si, Ge) atoms play subtle yet consequential roles in local backbone geometry and protein loop stabilization — particularly in conserved cysteine-rich motifs (CX4C / CxxC) and toxin scaffolds.

Because close interatomic contacts in macromolecular coordinates can easily arise from crystal packing noise, alternate conformations, or backbone proximity alone, our methodology pairs strict crystallographic quality gating with small-molecule CSD angular distributions and stochastic polymer null controls before interpreting any structural signature.

Research Progression & Core Contributions

End-to-end progression from quantum/crystallographic baselines to proteome-scale mining and open software

1. CSD Crystallographic Baseline (>1.2M Entries)

Executed a 6-stage CCDC Python API and SMARTS filtration cascade (R1 ≤ 0.05, zero disorder, non-polymeric, ≥4-bond separation) to partition divalent sulfur contacts in (θ, δ) space into out-of-plane H-bonds versus in-plane σ-hole chalcogen bonds (n = 259 S···O) and catalog N = 7,058 Group 15 pnictogen sub-vdW contacts.

2. PDB Survey & Ramachandran Dihedral Locking

Engineered quality-gated PDB pipelines (primary altloc, resolution ≤ 2.50 Å, mean B_iso ≤ 40 Å², CD-HIT NR90 clustering) across N = 8,636 CX4C loops and 1,997 sub-vdW pins, showing Sγ(i+5)···O(i+2) pinning reduces Case 1 Type II dihedral circular variance by 82.2% (>98% in Gly-locked dual turns).

3. Kinematic Polymer Null Models & Confounders

Built a vectorized 100,000-walk NeRF kinematic null model (97,589 clash-free backbones), demonstrating that conjoint σ-hole geometry is 188.8× enriched in venom neurotoxin contacts versus random packing (5.91× under proximity-gated Null Model II) and orthogonal to disulfide count.

4. ParamBrahma HPC & 214M AlphaFold v4 Census

Parallelized structural mining workflows on IISER Pune's ParamBrahma SLURM cluster (achieving a 60% runtime reduction), screening 214M AlphaFold DB v4 models (32.7M sulfur-proximal pairs) under strict pLDDT ≥ 70.0 gating and cataloging 329,764 candidate C-turns across 5,286 clusters.

5. ClinVar Variant Mapping & Solution NMR Bridge

Cross-referenced >14,000 human CX4C motifs against 10,518 ClinVar missense variants (such as INS p.Thr97Pro, BMPR2, FBN1), and designed an isosteric Cys→Sec + 13C/15N NMR validation protocol grounded in wet-lab purification and NMR training (JC Lab & CSIR–NCL, May–Aug 2023).

6. Open Scientific Software: MolBridge Platform

Unified all thesis detectors into MolBridge: NonCovalent Atlas — an open-access Streamlit UI and FastAPI platform mapping 15+ NCI families with cKDTree + Numba/Rust kernels (94.1× single-structure speedup on 4HHB at 100% recall) and provenance hashing.
