  • Audited multi-turn scientific and quantitative LLM completions for factual accuracy, step-by-step logical consistency, and subtle domain hallucinations across biology, chemistry, physics, and programming tasks.

Problem & Scientific Evaluation Context

Modern large language models routinely produce grammatically fluent scientific explanations that conceal fatal flaws underneath: fabricated citations, silently dropped edge conditions, unit mismatches, ungrounded biochemical mechanisms, or invalid intermediate steps that happen to guess a plausible-sounding summary.

Surface-level grading misses these failure modes. Effective RLHF supervision in STEM requires tracing every reasoning transition against primary domain references and executable checks — isolating the exact premise or computational step where a candidate model diverges from ground truth.
