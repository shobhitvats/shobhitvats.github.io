Who I Am

I'm Shobhit — a final-year BS-MS student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune, specialising in Computational Structural Biology. My work lives at the intersection of rigorous biomolecular research and pragmatic systems engineering.

At IISER Pune's Sai Lab, I investigate noncovalent σ-hole interactions (pnictogen, tetrel, and chalcogen bonding) and peptide motif conformation across crystal and proteome-scale structures, alongside building MolBridge and ParamBrahma HPC pipelines.

Alongside academic research, spending the past year across industry teams has shaped how I build for real-world adoption — developing an automated Comparator Analysis tool for Knowledge Base Verification & Validation (KB V&V) at Velsera, auditing frontier STEM LLM reasoning at Outlier, and working in the Founder's Office at Nucleovir Therapeutics (AIC–IISER Pune) on R&D operations and shared storage infrastructure.

Outside the lab and terminal, I play flute, guitar, and keyboard, create digital art, and have led campus operations for IISER Pune's annual flagship festival Karavaan (2,000+ attendees, ₹10L+ budget).

MS Thesis Evaluation — Prof. M. S. Madhusudhan

IISER Pune Academic ERP · Department of Biology · MS Thesis Expert Evaluation Report
Roll: 20211200 Candidate: Shobhit Vats Sharma Thesis Title: Understanding the Roles of Non-Covalent Interactions in Biomolecular Structural Motifs Thesis Expert: Prof. M. S. Madhusudhan (IISER Pune) Supervisor: Prof. Saikrishnan Kayarat (IISER Pune) Work Place: IISER Pune Recommendation: Accept (Exp Reco)

Expert Final Report

“In his thesis Shobhit Vats has produced an old-fashioned conformational study (this is a compliment) along the lines of GN Ramachandran and others. For his thesis, Shobhit has studied all instances of Cys-X4-Cys residues in protein structures. He has done this separately for structures in the PDB and in the Alphafold database. Further, Shobhit has created an analysis pipeline tool called MolBridge that analyses such sequences for non-colvalent sulphur mediated chalcogen bonding. What are these gamma-hole interactions? Shobhit has shown through meticulous analysis that and concluded that these sequences (when the Cys residues are disulphide bridged) take on specialised geometry (that have unique values on the Ramachandran map) and these values imply the chalcogen bonding between residues. His program MolBridge has been designed to detect such interactions given a structure in PDB format. The thesis has many nuances of such structures (what types of turns are favoured, how the alphafold models are biased, etc.). Shobhit has been very diligent about controlling for various factors (statistical tests have been used appropriately) and this makes for a very engaging thesis.”

Expert Mid-Year Report

“Shobhit Vats Sharma is working on different types of tetrel bonds in proteins. He has studied structures from the (small molecule) Cambridge Database and the PDB. His thesis has two major mains - a) Characterising these interactions in known structures and b) using the physical characteristics of these interactions to correct/characterise of predict similar interactions.”
